# C4 Trace Tables — Adjacency List Graph & Prim's MST

Generated by running `gsoo.algorithms.c4_prim.PrimRealDataTrace`, which loads the real
50-location, 100-road dataset straight out of a local Postgres database (via `Session.load()`,
using `sql/schema.sql` + `sql/seed/*.sql` — requires a local DB, see `CONTRIBUTING.md` /
team instructions for setup), builds an `AdjacencyListGraph`, and runs
`PrimMST.run(graph, startId, trace=true)`. The table below is copied directly from that run,
not typed by hand.

Dataset loaded: 50 locations, 100 road rows -> 98 unique edges (2 duplicate rows in the
seed data were correctly rejected by `hasEdge()`'s duplicate check: one exact repeat of
`EXT-H02-GATE-01`, one reverse-direction repeat of `INT-08-INT-14`).

## Table 2 — Small hand-verified example (mechanism check)

Before trusting the algorithm on real data, it was verified against a small 6-node example
worked out by hand:

| Step | From | To | Cost | Running Total |
|------|------|-----|------|----------------|
| 1 | A | C | 2 | 2 |
| 2 | C | D | 3 | 5 |
| 3 | D | E | 1 | 6 |
| 4 | E | F | 2 | 8 |
| 5 | A | B | 4 | 12 |

**Result:** `connected = true`, total cost = 12, matching the hand-worked expectation exactly.
Edges B-D (5), C-E (6), D-F (7) were correctly excluded since both endpoints were already
in the tree by the time they were considered.
